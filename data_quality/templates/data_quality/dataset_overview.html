Honesty rules it keeps: - dates are absolute ("2026-09-04"), never "2 days ago", "live" or "today"; - runs are "recorded": DataPact does not check incoming files itself yet; - a violation is decided by a person, in the admin. The AI can suggest a cause on the Investigate page; it never records one, and this page offers no Resolve action because the application has none.
